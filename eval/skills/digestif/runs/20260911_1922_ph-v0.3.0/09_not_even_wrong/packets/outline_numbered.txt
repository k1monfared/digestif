L002:     Summary: Unverifiable not-even-wrong claims persuade via investment, identity, and tribal proof, not evidence alone. (¶1, ¶2, ¶18)
L003:         - 1 Theme: Not-even-wrong ideas are too ill-constructed for correct-or-incorrect evaluation. (¶2)
L004:             - 1.1 Pauli popularized not-even-wrong for fundamentally flawed ill-constructed ideas. (¶2)
L005:             - 1.2 Example: At age 89, Fields and Abel winner Atiyah claimed Riemann solution stumping math since 1859. (¶3)
L006:             - 1.3 Atiyah proof lacked rigor, sat outside evaluation; leaders rhetoric functions similarly. (¶4, ¶5)
L007:         - 2 Theme: Financial, social, or ethical investment in visionaries triggers belief-reinforcing psychology. (¶6)
L008:             - 2.1 Financial stake incentivizes belief in vision; dissonance resolution, not just protection. (¶6)
L009:             - 2.2 Committed investors accept outside-expertise predictions; milestones ease scrutiny of bold futures. (¶7)
L010:             - 2.3 Cycle: financial to emotional investment to receptivity to further investment plus identity. (¶8)
L011:             - 2.4 Founder might pair verifiable product promises with grandiose unfalsifiable civilization claims. (¶9)
L012:         - 3 Theme: Popularity and outsider framing create tribal social proof amplified by platforms. (¶10)
L013:             - 3.1 Thousands or millions praising genius give social proof; humans follow others cues. (¶10)
L014:             - 3.2 Figures often pose as outsiders; questioning claims can feel like tribal betrayal. (¶11)
L015:             - 3.3 Algorithms tend to reinforce beliefs via echo chambers; seemingly unfiltered posts build sense of authenticity. (¶12)
L016:         - 4 Theme: We are not purely rational; interests, consistency, belonging, narratives shape evaluations. (¶13)
L017:             - 4.1 Aligned factors can override criticism, letting us accept claims we might otherwise scrutinize. (¶13, ¶14)
L018:             - 4.2 Atiyah was ultimately rejected; leaders often escape; society lacks comparable proof frameworks. (¶15)
L019:         - 5 Theme: Separate claim evaluation from investment via literacy, experts, frameworks, norms. (¶16, ¶17)
L020:             - 5.1 Individuals: separate finance from belief, check domains, seek counter-narratives; if invested ask Would-I-believe test. (¶16)
L021:             - 5.2 Society: teach tech literacy, platform experts, build frameworks, normalize skepticism without animosity. (¶17)
L022:             - 5.3 Judge claims independently between blind acceptance and reflexive rejection, honoring innovation and integrity. (¶18)
L023:             - 5.4 Evidence: Calling Bullshit covers statements directly; Surveillance Capitalism covers narrative-steering indirectly. (¶19)
