L002:     Summary: The matrix separates cognitive and affective empathy, sympathy, and compassion, then profiles psychopath, sociopath, empath, altruist, and integrated ideal. (¶1, ¶2, ¶8, ¶10)
L003:         - 1 Four components sit left of the double rule; five dark-to-light profiles sit right. (¶2)
L004:             - 1.1 Rows fall in four blocks: present in both, psychopath only, sociopath only, neither. (¶2)
L005:         - 2 Cognitive empathy means accurate modeling, boundary, and control; it survives in manipulators. (¶3, ¶6)
L006:             - 2.1 Evidence: The psychopath column is essentially the cognitive-empathy column standing alone. (¶8)
L007:             - 2.2 Nuance: Sympathy earns only partial on modeling: pity can badly misread people. (¶6)
L008:         - 3 Affective empathy means sharing emotion, with contagion, burnout, and vividness bias. (¶4, ¶7)
L009:             - 3.1 Nuance: Affective empathy lacks boundary maintenance, unlike everything else in the table. (¶7)
L010:             - 3.2 Nuance: Affective empathy earns only partial on caring: contagion can occur without concern. (¶6)
L011:             - 3.3 Evidence: The sociopath resembles affective empathy plus sympathy clamped to an in-group, unregulated. (¶8)
L012:         - 4 Compassion alone combines caring, motivation to help, wide scope, and sustainability. (¶4, ¶5)
L013:             - 4.1 Evidence: Only compassion scores full marks on helping motivation and sustainable scalable guidance. (¶4, ¶5)
L014:             - 4.2 Nuance: The Bloom and Singer pro-compassion case is a position, not a consensus. (¶7) [reported]
L015:         - 5 The psychopath keeps only cold instrumental control; the sociopath holds the affective cluster. (¶8)
L016:             - 5.1 Evidence: Almost all psychopath traits recur degraded in sociopath; only control is psychopath-only. (¶8, ¶3)
L017:             - 5.2 Evidence: Emotion-sharing, caring, helping, guilt, and contagion rows belong to sociopath, not psychopath. (¶4)
L018:         - 6 The empath maximizes affect unregulated, so it is not the moral ideal. (¶10)
L019:             - 6.1 Evidence: The empath lights up sharing, contagion, and bias but fails boundary, scope, and scalability. (¶10, ¶4)
L020:             - 6.2 The altruist embodies compassion and the Light Triad: Kantianism, Humanism, Faith in Humanity. (¶10)
L021:             - 6.3 The integrated ideal alone scores well across every block, opposing both dark profiles. (¶10)
L022:             - 6.4 Nuance: Kantianism inverts the manipulators row; only Faith in Humanity adds a new dimension. (¶10)
L023:         - 7 The table limits its scope deliberately and treats dark columns as ideal types. (¶9)
L024:             - 7.1 Nuance: Etiology stays out: causal origin is not a response shape. (¶9)
L025:             - 7.2 Nuance: Psychopath and sociopath are not formal diagnoses; both fall under antisocial personality disorder. (¶9)
L026:             - 7.3 Nuance: Terminology varies; empathic concern and pop-psych sympathy differ from table usage. (¶10)
