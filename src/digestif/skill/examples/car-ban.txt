Cities should ban cars from their centers. Some argue this hurts businesses, but studies of Madrid and Oslo show retail revenue rose after restrictions. Of course, buses and delivery vehicles must be exempt, otherwise goods do not reach shops. Critics also say such bans only work in wealthy cities. That may be true today, yet the same was said of bike lanes, which now succeed in Bogota and Jakarta. Public transit must expand first, though. A ban without transit is just displacement.
